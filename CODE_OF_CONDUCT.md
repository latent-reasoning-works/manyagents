# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give constructive, specific feedback,
welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and discuss ideas without
personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and
publishing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. These
standards apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at cesar.valdez@mila.quebec. Include
relevant context and links when available. Maintainers will review reports and
may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants according to the
severity and recurrence of the conduct. Reports and personal information should
be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
